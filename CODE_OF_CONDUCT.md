# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want LuxuryGram to be a welcoming, practical, and respectful open-source project. Participation is open regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Discuss code and decisions, not the worth of the person proposing them.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and help move the work forward.
- Protect private information and use responsible disclosure for security issues.
- Respect upstream projects, contributors, licenses, and trademarks.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, or publishing private information.
- Pressuring people to reveal credentials, account data, private conversations, or security details publicly.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces represented as part of LuxuryGram.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when possible, and focused on community safety.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content. For a sensitive report involving project maintainers, use [GitHub's private abuse-reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) and include links to the relevant content. Do not publish personal or security-sensitive evidence in a public issue.
